# Appstack

Modular paid-social system: swappable hooks and motion templates shipped weekly for performance teams under real throughput pressure.

## Scope

- Template architecture for paid social and UGC-style hooks
- Motion primitives that could be remixed without breaking brand guardrails
- QA-friendly naming and versioning for handoff to media buyers

## Approach

We optimized for **velocity**: short loops, predictable file structure, and motion rules that made “swap the hook, keep the physics” trivial for editors.

## Outcomes

- A library of approved transitions and text beats that scaled across SKUs
- Weekly ship cadence without visual drift between markets
- Fewer reshoots because motion carried performance learnings into the next batch
