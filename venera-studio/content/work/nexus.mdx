# Nexus

In-app motion language: reveals, transitions, and onboarding loops for a creator tool where retention hinges on the first session.

## Scope

- Core transition language for navigation and panels  
- Onboarding and empty states with a human, non-corporate voice  
- Micro-interactions for creation milestones and share flows  

## Approach

We kept motion **quiet until it mattered**: small spatial moves for everyday navigation, then bolder choreography only when the user shipped something worth celebrating.

## Outcomes

- A cohesive timing ramp so the app felt fast without feeling nervous  
- Reduced cognitive load in dense editing surfaces  
- Motion specs engineers could implement without bespoke timelines every sprint
