# Meta Rayban × Oakley

Campaign and product motion for the Meta eyewear collaboration, combining hero beats, UI-adjacent reveals, and retail energy tuned for performance media.

## Scope

- Launch hero and flagship social films
- Product-first loops for paid social and PDP-adjacent surfaces
- Motion kits for regional retail and partner channels

## Approach

We treated every asset as part of a **system**, not a one-off: shared grids, consistent type motion, and a single ramp for product reveals so the story stayed legible at three seconds and at thirty.

## Outcomes

- A modular cutdown ladder the team could ship weekly without drifting off-brand
- Motion guidelines the internal studio could extend after handoff
- A single visual language from hero film to in-feed product demo
