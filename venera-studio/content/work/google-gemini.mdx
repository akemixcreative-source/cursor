# Google Gemini

Launch and campaign motion for Gemini, spanning product story, UI beats, and hero films built for a fast-moving roadmap.

## Scope

- Hero and narrative films for flagship moments
- UI-centric sequences for product education and keynote
- Toolkits for internationalization and variant messaging

## Approach

Motion followed **product truth first**: every beat had to map to a real capability, then earn style. That kept cuts honest under scrutiny and fast to localize.

## Outcomes

- A reusable UI motion vocabulary for surfaces across web and in-product
- Cutdown logic that preserved clarity when aspect ratios and safe areas shifted
- A calmer, editorial rhythm that let dense UI read without fighting the frame
